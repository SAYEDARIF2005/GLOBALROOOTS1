Quality Assurance

Quality Begins at the Source

Every product supplied by GLOBAL ROOOTS undergoes a disciplined 7-stage quality journey from farm to port.

01

Farmer Sourcing

Direct engagement with growers across India’s core agricultural regions to select harvest batches.

02

Product Selection

Careful screening of raw yield based on physical parameters, moisture levels, and ASTA color value.

03

Quality Inspection

Multi-point visual evaluation testing for purity, aroma, cleanliness, and size uniformity.

04

Processing & Preparation

Controlled drying, sorting, grading, and cleaning to ready the products for international shipment.

05

Packaging

Packing in export-standard double jute sacks, Kraft paper bags, or food-grade moisture-barrier liners.

06

Final Quality Check

Pre-shipment batch inspection ensuring sealed container integrity and proper palletization.

07

Export & Delivery

Port clearance, phytosanitary & customs documentation, and international ocean vessel freight.
